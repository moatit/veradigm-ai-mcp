# Drawbridge launch campaign 1/6: positioning and messaging

Owner: Ali Khan (MOATiT). Drafted Oct 16, 2026. Nothing in this campaign has been sent; no one has been contacted.

## 1. Positioning statement

**For** independent and mid-sized medical practices that run **Veradigm® EHR and Veradigm® Practice Management**,
**who** lose front-desk hours every day to indexing faxed results and answering routine calls,
**Drawbridge**, MOATiT's AI technology for healthcare practices,
**files incoming faxes and lab results to the right patient's chart and answers the clinic line 24/7**,
**unlike** answering services, generic AI receptionists or offshore indexing teams,
**because** it works inside the practice's own EHR and schedule, verifies every patient on two identifiers before it files or shares anything, never guesses, and is built and run by MOATiT, an IT, phone and security company that has supported businesses and practices since 2013. Practices keep their own IT team or IT provider; MOATiT handles the Drawbridge setup with them.

**One-liner:** Lab results filed. Calls answered. Nothing guessed.

**Visionary hooks:** "Is your Veradigm® practice ready to be agentified?" · "Are you ready for the future of your practice?" (Veradigm® only as a compatibility reference, with the attribution line; never implying endorsement.)

**Tone:** positive and visionary, never scary. Drawbridge adds capacity; it is not a replacement for the practice's current system or its staff. It multiplies what they can do.

**Endorsed-brand line (always somewhere on every asset):** Drawbridge — A MOATiT Service.

## 3. Personas

### 3.1 Owner / physician (economic buyer)
- **Cares about:** patient safety, liability, staff turnover, cost per visit, results reaching the right provider.
- **Fears:** a result in the wrong chart; an AI giving medical advice; "another system".
- **Message:** "Results reach the right chart and the right provider, critical ones flagged, and nothing is ever guessed. It works inside the EHR you already have."
- **Proof to lead with:** 0 wrong-patient filings (synthetic), the drawbridge rules, no medical advice / 911 line, shadow week.
- **CTA:** Book a 20-minute walkthrough.

### 3.2 Practice manager / administrator (champion)
- **Cares about:** staff hours, phone hold times, after-hours coverage, onboarding effort, predictable cost.
- **Fears:** a long implementation; a big ask of their IT team or IT provider; staff rejecting it; hidden costs.
- **Message:** "About half of documents filed with no staff touch in our tests, the rest arrive pre-read for one click. A five-minute setup sheet for you, a short checklist for your IT team or IT provider, a week in shadow mode, and you decide when to switch on."
- **Proof:** ROI calculator, setup sheet, shadow week, "Contact us" pricing with everything included.
- **CTA:** Calculate your time savings → Book a walkthrough.

### 3.3 Front-desk lead (daily user, influencer)
- **Cares about:** the fax pile, the daily lab batch, calls stacking up during check-in, being blamed for misfiles.
- **Fears:** being replaced; cleaning up after a robot. (Address directly: Drawbridge is not a replacement; it gives them capacity.)
- **Message:** "Drawbridge isn't here to replace anyone. It takes the repetitive indexing and routine calls and hands you only the ones that need your judgment, already read, with the reason, so you have more time for the patients in front of you. You stay in charge of every uncertain document."
- **Proof:** the review-queue sample, "always to a person" documents, one-click file/correct.
- **CTA:** See a fax get filed.

### 3.4 IT / security (gatekeeper: the practice's own IT staff or outside IT provider)
- **Cares about:** BAA, MFA, encryption, audit, data location, least privilege, vendor risk questionnaires.
- **Fears:** PHI in logs; shared logins; data leaving the US; unknown subprocessors; a vendor that wants to take over their network.
- **What they're asked to do (a few permissions, no changes):** the practice administrator authorizes the Veradigm® activation and creates a dedicated Drawbridge user in Veradigm® EHR and PM; a small setting with the fax provider sends Drawbridge a copy of each incoming fax (number and line unchanged); read-only access to the folder the lab already uses (lab flow unchanged); the phone provider forwards the calls the practice chooses, or connects the assistant over SIP (number unchanged); an alert email. Nothing is installed on staff computers, and they keep running the practice's network, phones and PCs.
- **Message:** "You stay in charge of the practice's IT. Drawbridge is turnkey: a few permissions, no changes to how the practice works, and we do the rest. Named logins with mandatory MFA, a tamper-evident six-year audit log, server-enforced caller verification, minimum-necessary data, US hosting, an independent launch audit, and our security packet on request."
- **Proof:** trust center with status pills; launch-audit summary; vendor security questionnaire answers.
- **CTA:** Request the security packet.

## 4. Objection handling (short form)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "What if it files to the wrong patient?" | It needs name + DOB matching exactly one patient, and any SSN-4 or address on the page must agree. Look-alikes and conflicts go to a person. 0 wrong-patient filings in our synthetic testing; your shadow week measures your own documents before anything is filed. |
| "Patients won't like talking to AI." | It says it's the clinic's automated assistant, handles the routine requests quickly, and transfers to staff whenever asked. After hours it beats voicemail. |
| "We tried an answering service." | Answering services can't see your schedule. Drawbridge books, moves and confirms in Veradigm® PM, with a read-back. |
| "Is it HIPAA compliant?" | BAA with every practice; controls listed on the trust center, including what's still in progress. There's no official HIPAA certification, so we show our work instead. |
| "How long does setup take?" | It's turnkey: a five-minute questionnaire for the practice manager, a few permissions from your administrator and IT side, one shadow week, then switch on in stages. No new fax lines, no change to the lab flow. |
| "Will this replace my staff?" | No. It isn't a replacement for your system or your staff; it multiplies what they can do. Your team spends its time on patients and care while Drawbridge handles the repetitive indexing and routine calls. |
| "We already have an IT provider." | Good: keep them. Drawbridge doesn't replace your IT support. They grant a few permissions (a copy of incoming faxes, read-only lab-folder access, call forwarding or SIP, an alert email; your administrator creates the Drawbridge EHR/PM user) and we walk them through each. |
| "What does it cost?" | Quoted to your volumes; setup, shadow week, BAA and support included. |

## 5. Words to use / avoid

- **Use:** chart, fax, lab result, front desk, review task, ordering provider, shadow week, verify, read back, a person, your staff.
- **Avoid:** "replaces your staff / your front desk", "your new front desk", "fewer staff", "cut headcount", fear-based copy; scale multipliers ("10×") stated as fact or placed in proof/stats blocks (aspirational wording only, clearly framed); anything that assumes MOATiT already runs the practice's IT, phones or fax ("we already run your…", "as your IT provider", "we gather it ourselves"); "replace staff", "fully autonomous", "100% accurate", "HIPAA certified", "Veradigm partner"/"certified" (until true), "MCP", "FHIR", "Unity", "agentic", any client name.

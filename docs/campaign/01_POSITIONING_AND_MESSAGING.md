**For** independent and mid-sized medical practices that run **Veradigm® EHR and Veradigm® Practice Management**,
**who** lose front-desk hours every day to indexing faxed results and answering routine calls,
**Drawbridge**, MOATiT's AI technology for healthcare practices,
**files incoming faxes and lab results to the right patient's chart and answers the clinic line 24/7**,
**unlike** answering services, generic AI receptionists or offshore indexing teams,
**because** it works inside the practice's own EHR and schedule, verifies every patient on two identifiers before it files or shares anything, never guesses, and is run by a local IT, phone and security company that already looks after practices' systems.

**One-liner:** Lab results filed. Calls answered. Nothing guessed.

**Endorsed-brand line (always somewhere on every asset):** Drawbridge — A MOATiT Service.

## 3. Personas

### 3.1 Owner / physician (economic buyer)
- **Cares about:** patient safety, liability, staff turnover, cost per visit, results reaching the right provider.
- **Fears:** a result in the wrong chart; an AI giving medical advice; "another system".
- **Message:** "Results reach the right chart and the right provider, critical ones flagged, and nothing is ever guessed. It works inside the EHR you already have."
- **Proof to lead with:** 0 wrong-patient filings (synthetic), the drawbridge rules, no medical advice / 911 line, shadow week.
- **CTA:** Book a 20-minute walkthrough.

### 3.2 Practice manager / administrator (champion)
- **Cares about:** staff hours, phone hold times, after-hours coverage, onboarding effort, predictable cost.
- **Fears:** a long implementation; staff rejecting it; hidden costs.
- **Message:** "About half of documents filed with no staff touch in our tests, the rest arrive pre-read for one click. A five-minute setup sheet, a week in shadow mode, and you decide when to switch on."
- **Proof:** ROI calculator, setup sheet, shadow week, "Contact us" pricing with everything included.
- **CTA:** Calculate your time savings → Book a walkthrough.

### 3.3 Front-desk lead (daily user, influencer)
- **Cares about:** the fax pile, the daily lab batch, calls stacking up during check-in, being blamed for misfiles.
- **Fears:** being replaced; cleaning up after a robot.
- **Message:** "Drawbridge takes the routine crossings and hands you only the ones that need your judgment, already read, with the reason. You stay in charge of every uncertain document."
- **Proof:** the review-queue sample, "always to a person" documents, one-click file/correct.
- **CTA:** See a fax get filed.

### 3.4 IT / security (gatekeeper, often MOATiT itself)
- **Cares about:** BAA, MFA, encryption, audit, data location, least privilege, vendor risk questionnaires.
- **Fears:** PHI in logs; shared logins; data leaving the US; unknown subprocessors.
- **Message:** "Named logins with mandatory MFA, a tamper-evident six-year audit log, server-enforced caller verification, minimum-necessary data, US hosting, an independent launch audit, and our security packet on request."
- **Proof:** trust center with status pills; launch-audit summary; vendor security questionnaire answers.
- **CTA:** Request the security packet.

## 4. Objection handling (short form)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "What if it files to the wrong patient?" | It needs name + DOB matching exactly one patient, and any SSN-4 or address on the page must agree. Look-alikes and conflicts go to a person. 0 wrong-patient filings in our synthetic testing; your shadow week measures your own documents before anything is filed. |
| "Patients won't like talking to AI." | It says it's the clinic's automated assistant, handles the routine requests quickly, and transfers to staff whenever asked. After hours it beats voicemail. |
| "We tried an answering service." | Answering services can't see your schedule. Drawbridge books, moves and confirms in Veradigm® PM, with a read-back. |
| "Is it HIPAA compliant?" | BAA with every practice; controls listed on the trust center, including what's still in progress. There's no official HIPAA certification, so we show our work instead. |
| "How long does setup take?" | A five-minute questionnaire for the practice manager; MOATiT gathers the technical details; one shadow week; then switch on in stages. |
| "What does it cost?" | Quoted to your volumes; setup, shadow week, BAA and support included. |

## 5. Words to use / avoid

- **Use:** chart, fax, lab result, front desk, review task, ordering provider, shadow week, verify, read back, a person, your staff.
- **Avoid:** "replace staff", "fully autonomous", "100% accurate", "HIPAA certified", "Veradigm partner"/"certified" (until true), "MCP", "FHIR", "Unity", "agentic", any client name.

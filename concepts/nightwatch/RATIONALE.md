# Concept C: Night Watch

**In one line:** MOATiT at night. A dark-led, atmospheric page about the work Drawbridge does while the front desk is closed, built on MOATiT's shield.

## Why this direction

Drawbridge's most emotional benefit for a clinic owner is relief: the phone gets answered at 2 a.m. and the overnight faxes are read by morning. Night Watch makes that the mood. It's the most "premium" of the three and the most distinctive, while staying strictly inside MOATiT's colors.

## Logo

- **Mark:** MOATiT's shield with an arched gate cut through it and the gold deck lowered across the threshold. It's the closest family tie of the three: next to the MOATiT shield it looks like a sibling, which suits an endorsed brand.
- **Wordmark:** spaced capitals, drawn by hand at a light weight. It reads like architectural lettering and balances the solid shield.
- **Files:** `logo/lockup*.svg`, `mark*.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `wordmark.svg`. Every letter is a path, with no fonts.

## Palette

Night #1A1A33 (deepened from MOATiT ink, kept off violet) and MOATiT ink #222236 for alternating dark sections. One white section keeps the page grounded. Fog #F2F2FA and mist #AEB0D4 carry text on dark. MOATiT amber #F5A623 is the crossing (the button, the deck and the drawn line), and MOATiT blue #29AAE1 is the moat and product labels.

## Type

Poppins 500 for headlines (up to 104 px), Poppins 300 for the oversized "2:14 AM", and Barlow 500 for body.

## Page

- **Hero:** full height. "Only the right record crosses." sits bottom-left, with a line drawing of the shield-gate over the moat on the right.
- **After hours:** a sticky clock beside a log of a synthetic night: a lab result filed, a cancellation with read-back, an urgent call passed to on-call and a morning brief.
- **Security:** the one light section. A plain list of what MOATiT does: an agreement first, faxes read on MOATiT's servers, callers verified, every decision logged and a shadow week.

**Signature motion:** an amber line draws a record's path across the moat into the gate, the document arrives and the doorway warms. The water drifts very slowly. All motion is off with prefers-reduced-motion.

## Risks

Dark sites can read as "tech product" rather than "clinic partner", and some practice managers will scan it under office lighting on old monitors. The white security section and high-contrast text (fog on night is 15:1) are there to counter that. It also leans on one illustration, which needs a second and third drawing in the same hand before a full site.

MOATiT at night. Dark-led and atmospheric, about the work Drawbridge does when the front desk is closed, built on MOATiT's shield.

Type

Both faces are MOATiT's own: Poppins from the MOATiT documents and Barlow from moatit.com. Both are on Google Fonts.

Every fax in the right chart.

Poppins 500 / 300

Headlines at 500 and up to 104 px; the oversized clock at 300.

Drawbridge reads incoming faxes and lab results, matches each one to exactly one patient and files it. When it isn't sure, a person decides.

Barlow 500

Body on dark at 18 to 19 px, in Mist with Fog for emphasis.

Motion

The signature moment: an amber line draws a record's path across the moat and into the gate (2.4 s), the document arrives and the doorway warms. The water drifts very slowly. Sections fade up. All of it is off with prefers-reduced-motion.

Bans this direction keeps

No cream background, italic accent words, numbered mono labels, pill buttons, gradient text, emoji icons, glass cards, purple-to-blue gradients, or rows of identical cards. No AI photography.

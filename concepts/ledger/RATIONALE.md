# Concept B: Ledger

**In one line:** the product's own record turned into the brand. It's a clinical, structured page where the intake ledger is the hero and MOATiT blue leads.

## Why this direction

Clinic owners and practice managers trust what they can check. The strongest material on the launch site is the rules ("Two identifiers, one patient", "Never guess between patients"), and it was buried halfway down. Ledger leads with the rule as the headline and shows a morning's faxes going through it, including the three that went to a person and why. It looks like a well-made clinical tool, not a marketing site: hairlines, a strict grid, tabular figures and very little decoration.

## Logo

- **Mark:** a D drawn with MOATiT's rings: a navy post, an outer blue ring and an inner gold ring for the one controlled crossing. The concentric arcs come straight from the MOATiT logo, so the family tie is visible without the endorsement.
- **Wordmark:** the same hand-drawn geometric skeleton at a lighter weight in MOATiT ink. It sits on a thin blue rule, with "A MOATiT Service" beneath. The lighter weight is what separates this direction's voice from Gatehouse's.
- **Files:** `logo/lockup*.svg`, `mark*.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `wordmark.svg`. Every letter is a path, with no fonts.

## Palette

White 62%, ink #222236 for text and the dark phone section, callout blue #EAF6FC (MOATiT's own) for table heads and "filed" states, and MOATiT blue #29AAE1 as the lead accent (with blue 700 #0F6E9C for text on white). MOATiT gold #C68010 means exactly one thing: a person decides.

## Type

Poppins 500 for headlines, larger and tighter than A (up to 92 px, -0.04em), and Barlow 500 for body and data, with tabular figures.

## Page

- **Hero:** a two-line rule as the headline ("Two identifiers, or it doesn't file."), a short paragraph and a button. Below it is a full-width ledger of five synthetic faxes. Two are filed and three go to a person, each with the reason.
- **Decision flow:** three steps, ending in a fork: exactly one patient files; none, or more than one, goes to staff with the reason.
- **Phone:** a dark section with a timed transcript. The verification moment is highlighted, because nothing happens before it.

**Signature motion:** the ledger fills in row by row and each outcome stamps in after its checks, so the product's behavior is the animation. Everything else is quiet, and all motion is off with prefers-reduced-motion.

## Risks

It asks the reader to read. That suits practice managers, but it's less of a "brand moment" for a busy owner skimming on a phone. On narrow screens the ledger collapses to stacked entries, which works but loses some of its punch.

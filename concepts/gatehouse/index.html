The rules are written down and the same every time.

Drawbridge reads the page. Rules your team can audit decide where it goes. Every decision is logged with who, what and when.

The Drawbridge rulesApplies to every fax, lab result and call
RuleWhat happens
Two identifiers, one patient.Full name and date of birth must match exactly one patient. If they don't, the document goes to your staff queue with the reason.
Never guess between patients.Look-alikes, twins, a misread digit or a second patient on the page: a person decides.
Some documents always go to a person.Records requests, prior authorizations, refills and referrals are never filed automatically. Your practice sets the list.
Critical results get attention.A critical lab flag creates a high-priority review task with a phone call required.
Verify the caller first.Until a caller is verified on that call, no patient record can be read or changed.
Read it back. Wait for a yes.Every booking, move and cancellation is read back and made only after the caller agrees.

# Concept A: Gatehouse

**In one line:** the MOATiT document style turned into a product brand. White pages, navy type and gold used once per screen.

## Why this direction

Ali's MOATiT documents (the Idaho Tax SOW and the quotes) already look grown-up: Poppins, navy, blue and gold, white pages, navy section bars with a blue tab. Gatehouse takes that system, adds a mark and gives it room. A clinic owner who has seen a MOATiT proposal will recognize the family at once, and Drawbridge reads as MOATiT's product rather than a separate startup.

## Logo

- **Mark:** a D monogram. Its counter is the gate, and the gold deck runs out of it, across the bowl and past the letter. It carries one idea, the controlled crossing, where the launch mark tried to carry seven. It still reads as a D at 16 px. A channel cut around the deck keeps it legible in one color (fax cover sheets, embossing).
- **Wordmark:** "Drawbridge", drawn by hand on a Poppins-like geometric skeleton at a bold weight, in MOATiT navy. The SVGs contain no font files: every letter is a path, so they render the same everywhere.
- **Endorsement:** "A MOATiT Service", with MOATiT in capitals, the way MOATiT's own logo sets it.
- **Files:** `logo/lockup.svg`, `lockup-reverse.svg`, `lockup-mono.svg`, `mark.svg`, `mark-reverse.svg`, `mark-mono.svg`, `favicon.svg` (redrawn on a 32 px grid for 16 px), `wordmark.svg`.

## Palette

White 60%, MOATiT navy #3E3E84 20%, ink #222236 for text, pale navy #EEEEF8 for one tinted panel, MOATiT gold #C68010 only for the deck and rules (it fails contrast for small text on white, so it never carries words), amber #F5A623 for gold on navy, and MOATiT blue #29AAE1 for the document tab and focus states.

## Type

Poppins 600 for headlines (MOATiT documents) and Barlow 500 for body text (moatit.com). Both are MOATiT's own faces.

## Page

- **Hero:** an asymmetric split. On the left is a plain promise ("Every fax in the right chart. Every caller verified first.") with one button and a phone number. On the right is the mark with its deck lowering, and a filing slip for a synthetic fax.
- **Rules:** set as a MOATiT document table, with a navy bar, a blue tab and bold navy rule names.
- **Numbers:** labeled synthetic test results on a navy band, separated by rules instead of boxed in cards.
- **Going live:** agreement, shadow week and go-live on a single gold-marked track.

**Signature motion:** the deck lowers once on load. Everything else is a quiet fade-up, and all motion is off with prefers-reduced-motion.

## Risks

It's the safest of the three. Without the deck animation and the document table it could slide toward "generic B2B". The discipline that keeps it premium is gold only on the deck, one tinted panel per screen and no cards.
